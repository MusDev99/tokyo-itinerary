# PlanTravel product backlog

Owner view (2026-10-31): PlanTravel is strong for planning and preparing, but during the trip it only *informs*. The
backlog makes it something a traveller *uses* on the go: on a phone, under time pressure, often offline, often with others.

## Journey coverage
| Stage | Covered | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| 💭 Dream | Wishlist countries | — |
| 🗺️ Plan | Sheet-backed add/edit, analytics, currency | — |
| 🎒 Prepare | Checklist, calendar subscribe, PDF | Ticks are per device, not shared |
| ✈️ Travel | Today card, route, prayer times, offline PWA | Read-only: plans change, money is spent, things get skipped |
| 📸 Remember | — | Nothing after the trip |
